# Changelog

This changelog records changes to real board policies as the maintainer's research program verifies them. It exists to demonstrate what maintaining current policy records involves: rules change, guidance is superseded, and a record that was right in February can be wrong in July.

## Schema

### 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25
- Initial public draft: policy record schema, validator, five sample records, fixtures for every status path, reference consumer.

## Policy records

### 2026-11-25 — CRMLS fine position corrected
- The February 2026 CRMLS FAQ stated no fine applied to digitally altered image rule violations. The CRMLS MLS Citation Policy effective July 10, 2026 supersedes it: Rule 11.5.2 carries a $250 fine after a warning notice and a two day correction window. The old FAQ position is preserved as a superseded fixture (`fixtures/crmls-superseded.json`) because mistaking it for current guidance is a realistic failure.

### 2026-11-21 — HAR original image requirement resolved
- HAR staff confirmed the unaltered original is NOT required next to a virtually staged image, closing an open question in the record. Disclosure font and watermark confirmed acceptable.

### 2026-11-01 — NTREIS adopts an AI altered image rule
- NTREIS's rule took effect November 1, 2026, with an education period through January 31, 2027 and fines from February 1, 2027. Record in preparation for the maintained database; not among the five open samples.
